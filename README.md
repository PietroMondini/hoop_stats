# 🏀 hoop\_stats

A personal playground for experimenting with **machine learning, AI, and data-driven approaches** applied to NBA data.

This repository collects a series of small projects and explorations focused on extracting insights from basketball statistics, ranging from classical data analysis to more advanced AI techniques.

***

## 🚀 Overview

**hoop\_stats** is not a single application, but a collection of small experiments designed to explore new techniques or just data analysis.

The projects revolve around NBA data and include topics such as:

* NBA data dashboard
* Player similarity modeling
* Game outcome prediction
* Performance forecasting
* LLM-powered analysis tools

***

## 🧠 Goals

This repository serves mainly as a **learning and experimentation space**, with objectives such as:

* Trying out new ML/AI techniques in practice
* Comparing different modeling strategies
* Improving data engineering and feature design skills
* Building small, modular prototypes rather than polished products

***

## 📂 Structure

The repository is organized as a **collection of independent experiments**.

Each module or folder typically represents a specific use case or idea and its own dataset or preprocessing step or a modeling\analysis pipeline

***

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

This is a **personal project**:

* Code quality and structure may vary across experiments
* Some parts may be incomplete or exploratory
* Results are not intended as production-grade analytics

***

## 💡 Motivation

Basketball analytics offers a rich domain to explore:

* Complex, structured data
* Real-world predictive problems
* Opportunities to combine statistics, ML, and AI

This project is my way of learning by building.
